What Is an AI Prompt?

An AI prompt is an instruction or request given to an AI system. It tells the AI what you want it to do and can provide additional information such as context, tone, format, examples, or limitations.

For example, instead of writing:

"Write about digital marketing."

You could provide a more detailed instruction explaining the target audience, topic, tone, length, and structure.

The second approach gives the AI more information about the desired result.
